Our story

A quiet place
for big thoughts.

Greenwood Public Library began with a simple idea: a stronger community starts with shared knowledge. What began as a small neighborhood reading room has grown into a lively public space for readers, learners, makers, and families.

Today, our shelves hold thousands of books, but our purpose reaches much further. We create opportunities to learn new skills, discover different perspectives, and connect with people who share your interests.

Whether you visit for ten minutes or spend the whole afternoon, we want you to feel at home. Browse a new author, join a community event, or simply find a sunny spot and turn a page.

Our doors are open to all. Come as you are, bring your curiosity, and let the next chapter begin.
