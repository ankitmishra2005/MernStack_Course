Compound Interest Calculator

A simple demonstration of the compound interest formula using predefined JavaScript variables.

Formula

A = P (1 + r / n)nt

Compound Interest = A − P

Values used in the program

Principal (P)₹50,000
Annual rate (r)5%
Compounding (n)1 time/year
Time (t)3 years
Open the browser console (F12 → Console) to view the calculated amount and compound interest.
